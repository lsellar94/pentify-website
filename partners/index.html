What we build

The data layer your clients are missing

One data layer feeding every tool.

We connect the tools your clients already use into one warehouse, clean and join the data, then send richer data back into each one. Ad platforms bid on real revenue, the CRM knows which campaigns brought each deal, and reporting finally adds up.

  • No rip-and-replace: clients keep their tools
  • Richer signals back into ad platforms
  • Insight on top, from one set of numbers

A single view of every customer.

Each system holds a fragment of the customer: the website sees a visit, the CRM sees a deal, the help desk sees a ticket. Joined in the warehouse, they become one profile your client can act on, like fixing an open billing issue before offering a renewal.

  • Identity, engagement, value and service in one record
  • Next-best actions your client's team can trust
  • Audiences that go back into ad platforms

Every client's results, in one place.

The same foundation works for your own business. See performance across every client in one view, get alerts when a campaign drifts, and know which accounts actually make you money.

  • Cross-client performance view
  • Automated reports and pacing alerts
  • Client profitability, not just billings
